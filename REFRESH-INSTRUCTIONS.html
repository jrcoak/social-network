How to Hard Refresh Your Browser

Windows/Linux:
Press Ctrl + Shift + R
OR press Ctrl + F5
Mac:
Press Cmd + Shift + R
Alternative Method:
1. Open DevTools (press F12)
2. Right-click the refresh button
3. Select "Empty Cache and Hard Reload"
